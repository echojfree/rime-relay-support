Rime Relay — Privacy Policy

PRIVACY POLICY
Effective date: 2026-10-13

Rime Relay is an offline single-player game. The game does not send personal data, device identifiers, gameplay records or location to the developer. It includes no advertising, analytics, tracking, account system or in-app purchases.

ON YOUR DEVICE
Scores, progress, daily challenge history and sound/haptic preferences are stored locally. Daily challenges use the device's UTC date; no server is contacted. Current runs are not restored after the app is terminated. Deleting the app (not offloading it) removes its local data. Device backups managed by Apple may retain copies according to your settings.

SUPPORT
Contact echojayecho@gmail.com. If you email support, your address, message and any attachments are processed to answer your request. Do not send passwords or sensitive information. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable obligations; you may request deletion by email. Email providers process messages under their own terms.

APPLE SERVICES
App Store purchases, refunds, optional operating-system diagnostics and device backups are managed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. This game does not operate its own cloud save or telemetry service.

CHANGES
This policy will be updated if the game's data practices change. Contact: echojayecho@gmail.com.
