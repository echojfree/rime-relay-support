Rime Relay support · Privacy

Rime Relay

Freeze, kick and chain your way through a landscape platform arcade.

Support

Email echojayecho@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, game version and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information before sending.

Controls

Drag the left pad to move. Tap JUMP to jump. Hold SNOW to freeze enemies; tap SNOW near a frozen enemy to kick. Pause to adjust sound and haptics.

Progress

Records, unlocks and preferences are saved on your device. There is no account or cloud save. An unfinished run does not resume after the app is terminated. Deleting the app removes local progress; offloading may keep it.

Purchases

For App Store billing or refund requests, use Apple's purchase support.
